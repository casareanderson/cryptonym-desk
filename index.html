Why a digraph

Real CIA cryptonyms are a two-letter prefix naming the sponsoring office, then an arbitrary word: AEDINOSAUR, QKACTIVE, LIENVOY. The word is chosen to mean nothing. Yours is cut from what you gave the desk.

How the alias is cut

A working alias is a mashup, not a pick: the desk splits your names at their vowel groups and grafts the front of one onto the tail of another, so it reads like a person rather than a reference.

Nothing leaves the page
